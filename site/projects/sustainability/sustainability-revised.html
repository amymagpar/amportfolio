The Design Challenge

Cisco launched new sustainability-focused data center products but lacked customer-facing information about their environmental benefits. Marketing copy existed, but actionable technical data remained trapped in engineering silos.

User research revealed a critical gap: only 20% of customers and sales teams knew where to find sustainability data or were aware of Cisco's circular economy programs. For a company committed to environmental leadership, this wasn't just an information gap—it was a massive missed revenue opportunity.

Discovery: Uncovering the Hidden Opportunity

Research Approach

Conducted cross-functional stakeholder interviews across 6 departments to understand internal workflows, customer needs, and data silos. Combined qualitative insights with behavioral analytics to validate hypotheses.

Product Engineering Manager, Sustainability

"I have two RFPs from sales due in 48 hours requiring power usage metrics. I spent a day just trying to find if we have this data, only to discover it would take weeks to compile. We will lose the bids."

This story crystallized the core problem: not a lack of data, but an architectural failure making critical information inaccessible when needed most.

Data Validation: Behavioral Analysis

Created sustainability-interest cohort in Adobe Analytics (organic search terms, content engagement patterns, B2B segments) to validate the hypothesis with quantitative evidence.

Key Insight

Customers weren't ignoring sustainability—they couldn't find the pathway from "why sustainability matters" to "which Cisco products enable it." This was an information architecture problem, not a content problem.

Strategic Framework: Three-Pillar Solution

1

Solve the Architectural Problem

Problem: Sustainability data siloed in engineering dashboards, inaccessible to sales/marketing/customers—costing deals

Design Decision: Create AI-powered data extraction system, not just better navigation

What I Built: Conversational AI prototype querying complex product database, reducing data retrieval from weeks to minutes

Impact: RFP response time: 3 weeks → hours

2

Create the Content Pathway

Problem: High-intent visitors engaging with thought leadership (TOFU) but no pathway to products (BOFU)

Design Decision: Integrate sustainability data into product pages (contextual), not separate sustainability hub (siloed)

What I Built: Modular sustainability components (certifications, energy specs, lifecycle info) integrating into 200+ product pages, datasheets, comparison tools

Impact: 200% increase in sustainability content engagement

3

Enable Self-Service Discovery

Problem: Only 20% awareness of circular economy programs—existing capability, hidden from customers

Design Decision: Surface lifecycle programs at point of need (product research, upgrade flows), not separate portal

What I Built: Interactive filtering, comparison tools, visual end-of-life program indicators embedded in product discovery

Impact: Program awareness: 20% → 55%

Strategic Rationale

Prioritized #1 (AI dashboard) to solve systemic blocker enabling everything else. #2 captured high-intent traffic already on site (low customer acquisition cost). #3 provided quick win building stakeholder confidence.

AI-Powered Data Extraction: From Weeks to Minutes

The Technical Challenge

Sustainability data existed in complex product database but required manual extraction, multiple legal approvals, and 2-3 weeks to compile for customer-facing use.

Strategic Decision: Rather than redesigning entire dashboard (expensive, long timeline), I prototyped conversational AI solution to validate technical feasibility before requesting engineering investment.

Design Artifacts Created:

  • → Conversational flow diagrams

Architecture & Key Design Decisions

Multi-turn Dialog for Disambiguation

Sales query: "Show me power consumption for Catalyst" → System: "Which series? 9200, 9300, 9400?" → Refined response

Embedded Governance Workflow

Legal/brand approval built into flow before external sharing—trust signal showing "verified for customer use"

Graceful Error Handling

When data unavailable: show last known value with timestamp + escalation path to human review

Smart Caching Strategy

Common queries cached for performance, partner-specific data real-time—balancing speed vs. freshness
